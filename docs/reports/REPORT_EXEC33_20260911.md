### Reporting procedures: no choice made

(a) One globally fixed index: existing fixed-N evaluations support this output, but do not establish an independent global selection policy. (b) Same-sample per-position argmin: explicitly implemented in C3 and optimization scripts; the reported minimum is selection-biased downward.
