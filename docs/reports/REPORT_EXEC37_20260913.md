## Limits and configuration discrepancies

- D0/D3 cover **x=0 only, N=2000**. There is no END-only position scan; conclusions against test beam are confined to the centre. No statistical extrapolation to the full bar is made.
- **Sensor/PDE:** the meeting wording mixes “SiPMs Broadcom (FBK NUV-MT 14M)” at OV=10 V; the simulation PDE is AFBR-S4N66P024M, Broadcom, annotated at 12 V above breakdown. This affects the predicted photon budget and remains unresolved.
- **Time reference:** older CH8 FastIC+ trigger timing included 20–50 ps paddle jitter; recent detector-channel differences remove that reference from the observable. The new same-end difference matches that construction; no CH8 jitter is added.
- **Quantization:** test-beam TDC is 24.4 ps LSB, approximately 7.0 ps in quadrature per timestamp under a uniform quantization model. Simulation timestamps are not quantized; no correction is applied.
- **Reflector nomenclature:** the physical reflector is Vikuiti 3M ESR, R=0.98; Mylar is inherited naming, not a simulation/test-beam hardware discrepancy. D0/D3 differ in their archived optical boundary implementation, as requested.
- All relevant master seeds are 26092601 and 8349041. Shared-seed comparisons and left/right results are not assumed independent; no BLUE, arm average or sigma(x) fit is performed.
