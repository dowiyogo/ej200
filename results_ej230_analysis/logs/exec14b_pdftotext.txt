Why does tn disperse? Decomposition into four mechanisms

1. Order statistics on the emission tail (irreducible): tn are
order statistics of Npe draws from the emission pdf (τr = 0.5
ns, τd = 1.5√ns); successive gaps ∼ Exp(τd /(Npe − n + 1)),
σstat (tn ) ≈ n τd /Npe for n  Npe — grows monotonically
with n even with perfect optics.
2. Landau Npe fluctuations:
VarL (tn ) = (∂htn i/∂Npe )2 Var(Npe ) with Var(Npe ) from the
documented F = 1 + chNpe i. Statistical but non-Poissonian.
3. Optical path-length dispersion: TIR photons at n = 1.58
(θc ≈ 39◦ ) span axial speeds c/(n cos θ) → 1.4–1.5 ns/m per
metre plus Mylar bounce tail (R = 0.98). Low-n photons:
quasi-direct; high-n: reflection-dominated ⇒ RMS ∝ n.
4. Attenuation truncation: distant channels (λeff ≈ 30.8 cm)
populate only n = 1–3 with large purely statistical bars.

Takeaway

√
RMS(tn ) − n τd /Npe isolates the
optical-propagation contribution — the
per-channel diagnostic in the htn i
dispersion frames and the same dispersion
that limits σgroup .

Why Landau (and why Moyal as analytic proxy)
Why not Gaussian:
I In 10 mm of plastic the muon undergoes
many collisions, but the energy-transfer
cross-section (dσ/dε ∝ 1/ε2 ,
Rutherford/Møller) has a hard tail.
I Rare large transfers (δ-rays) dominate the
variance; the variance per collision diverges
and the Central Limit Theorem does not
apply.
I Result: asymmetric distribution with a hard
right tail — the Landau distribution.
I Gaussian (Vavilov→Gauss) limit only applies
to thick absorbers.

Why Moyal:
I Landau density has no closed form (contour
integral).
I The Moyal function is a closed-form
analytic approximation to the Landau core
and early tail, enabling stable MINUIT fits.
I Known limitation: extreme Moyal tail
undershoots true Landau tail; fit quality
degrades at high Npe .

Moyal ⊗ Gauss:
I Photo-electron counting (Poisson) and
collection fluctuations broaden the peak
symmetrically.
I Full model: Moyal ⊗ Gaussian.
I Connects to Fano slide: same Landau ∆E
fluctuation, two observables (spectrum
shape + super-Poissonian F ).

(6 × 6 mm2 ) in the +Y Mylar face.
I First-pass photon collection depends on the solid
angle subtended by the window from the muon
track (geometric working hypothesis, consistent
with five-run pattern).
I Empirically, a track at ±2 mm from the window
centre changes the local contrast by 7.9% with
minimum significance 9.3σ. The measured centered

-0.06σ, respectively.
I Scan positions with x ≡ 10 (mod 20) mm fall at
±2 mm from a window centre — hence the
“(window-track exception)” label in the tables.
I Effect is local, symmetric, characterised and
bounded: localization diagonal and channel map
remain valid.
