The preregistered H1 treats this as a lower bound on aggregate first-encounter escape, and passes only if the measured fraction minus three SE is at least 0.226172.

The measured aggregate is 10,140,104 escapes among 39,471,287 first exiting scintillation encounters:

0.256898236 ± 0.000070760; lower 3-SE limit = 0.256685956 > 0.226172

## V1 accounting observation

The existing N=2,000 run produced 40,039,535 scintillation photons. Deposited energies were 3,848.966433 MeV total, 60.828217 MeV from optical photons, 3,788.138216 MeV from non-optical particles, and zero recorded non-ionizing deposit. Event-level bookkeeping closes exactly for `ionizing + nonionizing = total`.

Using only the physically intended non-optical denominator gives the retained EXEC_40 result:

```text
40,039,535 / (10,400 ph/MeV * 3,788.138216 MeV)
  = 1.01631859 ± 0.00016034
```

This is not a changed material yield. The runtime log records OPSC-101, 10,400 ph/MeV and `RESOLUTIONSCALE=1`, with no active Birks material printed. In the installed Geant4 11.4 source, however, `G4Scintillation::IsApplicable` returns true for every non-short-lived particle (`G4Scintillation.cc:150-153`), and `G4OpticalPhysics` attaches scintillation to every applicable particle (`G4OpticalPhysics.cc:151-175`). Thus absorbed optical-photon energy can scintillate again. The process computes yield times visible deposited energy and applies Gaussian/Poisson fluctuations (`G4Scintillation.cc:278-347`).

At 10,400 ph/MeV, the measured optical deposit predicts 632,613.46 additional photons. The observed excess over the non-optical expectation is 642,897.55 photons, so optical re-scintillation accounts for 98.4004%. The remaining 10,284.09 is only 1.61 times the event-bootstrap uncertainty implied by the all-deposit ratio `1.00025691 ± 0.00015948`, and is compatible with `RESOLUTIONSCALE=1`. The effective yield against all model-eligible deposited energy is 10,402.67 ph/MeV. The V1 excess is therefore resolved as a denominator/model-applicability effect, not Birks, a changed nominal yield, or duplicate counting by the observation counter. No physics parameter was modified.
